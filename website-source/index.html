THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Everything you might want to know before choosing your next lock.

Are the prices shown final? +

No. Prices displayed are reference prices observed on Godrej's public online listings, not live quotes from Poonam Enterprises. Contact us to confirm the current local price and availability.

Can I enquire about installation or repairs? +

Yes. Use the service enquiry form to prepare the details of your request. Direct WhatsApp sending will become available once the business contact number is configured.

How do I know which lock fits my door? +

Door material, thickness, direction of opening and your preferred type of access matter. Share these details in your enquiry so a suitable option can be recommended.

Where can I verify specifications and availability? +

Each product includes a link to its official Godrej listing, where you can check the manufacturer's latest published information.
